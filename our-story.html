HOW IT STARTED

I couldn’t find a tracker
I actually liked.

The ones I tried were clunky, incomplete, and asked for too much manual entry. I wanted one place to save the strain, brand, batch, terpenes, flavor, effects and whether I would buy it again.

One night, I decided to build it myself. The goal was simple: keep the details that matter later, and make recording them easier.

From the person building Strain Diary

THE IDEA BEHIND EVERY FEATURE

“A diary should help you
remember the experience.
Not feel like homework.”

Less repetitive typing. More useful notes. A place to come back to.

WHAT I’M BUILDING TOWARD

Useful today.
Better tomorrow.

The small things matter when you’re using an app again and again.

01

Remember the good jars.

Keep product details, photos and your own observations together, so an old favorite is more than a name you almost remember.

02

Make logging easier.

Label scanning and voice notes help cut down on repetitive typing. Spend more time on the details you actually want to save.

03

Listen. Fix. Repeat.

Real feedback helps me find what’s confusing, what’s missing, and what takes more effort than it should.
