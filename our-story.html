Why I built it

I didn’t like the other trackers, so I made my own.

Every strain tracker I tried felt awkward to use, was missing information or made me type in way too much. None of them remembered the details I actually wanted later.

It started with one simple problem

I wanted to remember which jar was the good one. Not just the strain name—the brand, batch, terpenes, flavor, how I used it and whether I would actually buy it again.

One night after taking some dabs, I had the idea that I could probably build a better tracker myself. Then I started building it.

Built by someone who actually uses it

Strain Diary is independently built and maintained by one person. I use it, I read the feedback and I make the changes. That is why the app focuses on fast logging, real product data and a layout that does not get in your way.

Where it is going

The goal is to make Strain Diary the ultimate cannabis companion app: label and COA scanning, terpenes, crosses, discovery, education, device controls, social features and chats in one place, with privacy built in from the start.

It is still growing, and testers have already shaped a lot of it. If you have an idea or something feels wrong, tell me.
