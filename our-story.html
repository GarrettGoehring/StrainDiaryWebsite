WHY I BUILT IT

I couldn’t find a strain tracker I actually liked.

The ones I tried were clunky, incomplete, and asked for too much manual entry. I wanted an easier way to remember which jar was worth buying again.

HOW IT STARTED

One simple problem became the app I wanted.

I wanted one place to save the strain, brand, batch, terpenes, flavor, effects and whether I would buy it again. One night, I decided to build a better tracker—one that handles as much of the data entry as possible.

“The details that matter later, without turning every entry into homework.”
