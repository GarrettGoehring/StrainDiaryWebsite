Why I built it

I couldn’t find a strain tracker I actually liked.

The ones I tried had clunky layouts, missing strain data and way too much manual entry. I just wanted an easy way to remember which jar was actually worth buying again.

“I wanted the details that matter later, without turning every entry into homework.”

Built independently by someone who uses it

How it started

One simple problem became the app I wanted.

01

Remember the good jars

I wanted one place to save the strain, brand, batch, terpenes, flavor, effects and whether I would buy it again.

02

Make logging easier

One night after taking some dabs, I decided to build a better tracker—one that handles as much of the data entry as possible.

03

Keep improving it

I use the app, read the feedback and make the changes. If anything feels confusing or harder than it should be, I want to fix it.

Where it’s going

Everything I want from a cannabis app, in one place.

The long-term goal includes label and COA scanning, strain data, terpenes, crosses, discovery, education, connected-device controls, social features and chats—with personal information private by default.
