Find the strain you remember liking.

A strain tracker saves more than a name. In Strain Diary, each entry can include the brand, product type, photos, effects, flavor, rating and session notes. Search your past entries when you want to remember a product or compare it with something new.

For example, two jars with the same strain name can have different label details. Keeping separate product records lets you compare what you actually logged instead of treating every jar as identical.

From package to searchable entry

  1. Add the product. Scan a testing label or a COA QR code, use voice entry, or add the details yourself. Review scanned values before saving.
  2. Add your observations. Record your rating, flavor, effects and a short note. A few consistent details are easier to compare than a long entry you rarely finish.
  3. Revisit and compare. Search by strain, brand, product type or rating. Review recorded terpene profiles alongside your notes.

A strain library and your personal history

Strain Diary includes more than 1,000 strain reference profiles and tracks 20 terpenes. Reference information helps organize the names and label details you encounter. Your journal adds the part a reference library cannot supply: your own record of a specific product and experience.

Recorded ratings and terpene profiles are tools for reviewing observations, not guarantees of how another product will feel.

Private by default, with sharing you control

Your strain journal is private by default. The app also includes Friends and Global feeds for entries you choose to share. Read the privacy policy to understand how data is handled.

Strain tracker questions

Is Strain Diary available on iPhone?

Yes. Free early access is available on iPhone through Apple TestFlight while the App Store release is being prepared. The download page explains installation.

Can I track concentrates as well as flower?

Yes. Strain Diary supports flower, pens and concentrates, with product details, ratings and session notes.

How is a strain tracker different from a cannabis journal?

A tracker organizes product details so they can be searched and compared. A journal captures your personal observations. Strain Diary combines both. Our cannabis journal guide includes a simple template for getting started.
