BEFORE YOUR FIRST ENTRY

A few things
worth knowing.

New to keeping notes? Try our cannabis journal guide and template.

Is Strain Diary available on iPhone?

Yes. Free early access is available on iPhone through Apple TestFlight while the App Store release is being prepared. The download page explains installation.

Can I track concentrates as well as flower?

Yes. Strain Diary supports flower, pens and concentrates, with product details, photos, ratings and personal notes.

How is a strain tracker different from a journal?

A tracker organizes product details so they can be searched and compared. A journal captures your personal observations. Strain Diary combines both.

Can my ratings predict how a strain will feel?

Your ratings and recorded terpene profiles help you review past observations. They cannot guarantee future effects or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
