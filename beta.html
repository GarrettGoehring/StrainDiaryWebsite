FAQ

Before you install

Is my data private?

Yes. There are no ads or third-party trackers, and your information is not sold. Your private diary stays private unless you deliberately share something through a social feature.

Is it free?

The beta is completely free. Your feedback will help shape Strain Diary before its official launch.

Do I need an account?

Only for the social side. You can skip sign-up during onboarding and use the diary, scanner, terpene charts and Discover on their own. You can create an account later if you want to use friends or chats.

How does the scanner work?

Point your camera at the testing label on a jar or pen. Strain Diary reads details such as the strain name, type, THC percentage and terpene list, then fills in the entry form for you. You can also enter the information yourself.

Where does the strain data come from?

The app includes a built-in library of classic, landrace, trending and California-popular strains, including known crosses and terpene profiles. If a strain is not already there, you can add it yourself.

Can I export my diary?

You can share any strain as a card, and full export is on the list. If you need it sooner, say so—testers’ requests move up the roadmap.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Strain Diary is iPhone-only while the App Store launch is the priority.

How do I report a bug?

In TestFlight, take a screenshot and tap “Share Beta Feedback.” You can also email support@straindiary.com or use the contact page.
