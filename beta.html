BEFORE YOU INSTALL

Frequently asked questions.

Is my data private?

Diary entries are not shown to other users by default. The Strain Diary privacy policy explains storage, sharing, and access for support or moderation.

Is it free?

Yes. TestFlight early access is completely free.

Do I need an account?

Only for social features. You can use the diary, scanner, terpene charts and Discover without signing up.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Strain Diary is iPhone-only while the App Store launch is the priority.

How do I report a bug?

Use “Share Beta Feedback” in TestFlight or contact us about a bug.
