Free iPhone beta

Try Strain Diary before it hits the App Store.

The full beta is available through Apple’s TestFlight. Use the app normally, and if something feels confusing, breaks or is missing, let me know.

Open in TestFlight

How to install it

  1. Install Apple’s free TestFlight app if you do not already have it.
  2. Tap “Open in TestFlight” above and accept the invitation.
  3. Install Strain Diary and start logging.

What you are getting

This is the actual app, not a stripped-down demo. You can use the diary, scanner, terpene charts, discovery, social features, chats and built-in browser while they are still being improved.

What I need from testers

There is no formal testing checklist. If you notice a bug, a label that scans wrong or a feature that would make the app easier to use, send it through TestFlight or the contact page. Good or bad, I want to hear it.

A quick heads-up

Beta builds can have bugs and features may change before release. Strain Diary is for adults of legal cannabis age and is not medical advice.
