BEFORE YOU INSTALL

Frequently asked questions.

Is my data private?

Yes. There are no ads or third-party trackers, and your information is not sold. Your diary stays private unless you deliberately share something.

Is it free?

The beta is completely free.

Do I need an account?

Only for social features. You can use the diary, scanner, terpene charts and Discover without signing up.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Strain Diary is iPhone-only while the App Store launch is the priority.

How do I report a bug?

Use “Share Beta Feedback” in TestFlight or visit the contact page.
