Strain Diary competitions

Competitions

Strain Diary competitions let organizers set up entries and invite judges to rate them in the app. Everyone can follow judging progress live, while scores and winners stay hidden until the host reveals them.

For organizers

Host a competition

Create categories and entries, invite judges with a code or QR label, and control when judging closes and results are shared.

  1. Install Strain Diary on your iPhone.
  2. Open Settings → Competitions and create your event.
  3. Share the competition code with judges and viewers.

For judges & viewers

Follow a competition

Enter the code from your poster or organizer. Watch the live judging progress, or open the join page to score entries in the app.

4–8 letters or numbers, no spaces.

A free Strain Diary account is required to judge. The live display is for viewing; the host approves presenter controls separately in the app. Results remain under wraps until the host releases them.
