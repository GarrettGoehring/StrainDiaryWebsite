Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Your privacy matters. Strain Diary is designed to keep your personal diary data private and under your control. Your private strain logs, reviews, ratings, and notes are stored through your personal iCloud account. Strain Diary does not sell your personal information, use your data for advertising, or include third-party advertising or tracking services. Some features, such as Community and Chat, involve sharing information with others. Content is only made publicly visible when you choose to share it publicly. Your private strain entries remain private and are not visible to other users.

The short version

    • Your personal strain diary is stored in your iCloud account and synced using Apple's CloudKit.
    • Other users cannot see your private strain entries.
    • Posts are only visible to the community when you choose to make them public.
    • Community, Chat, and Diary content may be accessible to Strain Diary's owner or authorized moderators when necessary for moderation, support, safety, or operation of the service.
    • General information about new strains you add may be contributed to Strain Diary's shared strain database, but your personal reviews, ratings, and notes are not.
    • Your data is never sold or rented to advertisers.
    • There are no third-party advertising networks or tracking SDKs.
    • You can edit or delete your diary entries at any time.
    • Strain Diary is intended for adults of legal cannabis age only.

What Strain Diary stores

Strain Diary stores the information you choose to put into it. This may include strains you save, ratings and reviews, notes, effects, terpenes, photos you attach, and any session or consumption details you choose to record. There's no separate Strain Diary account you need to create for your personal diary. The app uses the iCloud account already signed in on your device. Your personal diary information is stored using Apple's CloudKit and associated with your personal iCloud account. Community and Chat features work differently because certain information needs to be shared or synced in order for those features to function.

This information is written to your private CloudKit database, which lives inside your personal iCloud. It's the same place your other iCloud app data sits, protected by your Apple Account.

How iCloud and CloudKit work here

CloudKit is Apple's storage system. Your entries sync between your own devices through it, so what you log on your phone shows up on your other Apple devices signed in to the same iCloud account. Apple, as the infrastructure provider, handles that storage and transfer under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple, as the infrastructure provider, handles the underlying storage and transfer of iCloud data under Apple's applicable privacy practices and terms.

Strain Diary was designed to use iCloud so your personal diary can remain associated with your Apple Account rather than requiring Strain Diary to maintain a traditional account containing all of your personal diary information.

Community & Public Posts

Strain Diary includes social and community features that allow you to share content, discover strains, interact with posts, and connect with other users.

When you use Community features, Strain Diary may store and process the following information:

  • Username and profile information — Used to identify you to other users and display your profile alongside posts, comments, reactions, and other social activity.
  • Profile photo — Used to personalize your profile and identify your account throughout Community and Chat features.
  • Posts and other content you share — Used to display the content you choose to publish or share with the Strain Diary community.
  • Strain information you share — Used to display strains you intentionally share through Community features and provide relevant strain details to other users.
  • Comments and replies — Used to allow conversations and interactions between users on Community content.
  • Reactions and other interactions — Used to provide features such as likes, reactions, and other ways of interacting with Community content.
  • Photos you share — Used to display images you intentionally include with Community posts or other social content.

What I can and can't see

Strain Diary does not collect personal profiles, contact lists, location trails, or information for the purpose of building an advertising profile about you.

Your personal diary data is stored through iCloud and is not made available to other Strain Diary users. Strain Diary's owner or authorized moderators may access Community, Chat, and Diary content when necessary for moderation, support, safety, troubleshooting, or operation of the service.

If Apple provides anonymous or aggregate usage, crash, or diagnostic information to help identify problems with the app, that information does not include the contents of your personal strain journal.

If you've enabled Share iPhone Analytics in your iOS settings, Apple may provide developers with diagnostic, crash, and performance information. You can manage your analytics preferences through the Privacy & Security settings on your device.

Connected devices

If you pair Strain Diary with a compatible device, that connection happens locally between your phone and the hardware. 

The connection is used to provide the device feature you requested. Information about that local pairing is not sold to advertisers or used to create an advertising profile about you.

When you reach out

If you contact Strain Diary directly or send feedback through TestFlight, I may receive the information you choose to include in your message.

TestFlight reports may also include technical information provided by Apple, such as your device model, operating system version, crash information, or other diagnostic details.

This information is used to respond to your message, troubleshoot problems, improve Strain Diary, or address the issue you reported.

Your support messages are not used to add you to a marketing mailing list or sold to third parties.

What Strain Diary does not do

Strain Diary does not sell or rent your personal information.

There are no third-party advertising networks, cross-app advertising trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs quietly building an advertising profile based on how you use your diary.

Your diary is intended to be a place to record your experiences — not a source of advertising data.

Age requirement

Strain Diary is intended only for adults who are of legal age to purchase cannabis where they live.

It isn't directed to children or anyone under the applicable legal cannabis age.

Strain Diary is a personal logbook and informational tool. It does not provide medical advice. For health-related questions or decisions, you should consult an appropriately qualified professional.

Children's privacy

You can edit or delete your personal diary entries directly within Strain Diary, and applicable changes can sync across your devices through iCloud.

Strain Diary does not knowingly collect personal information from children or knowingly allow minors to use features intended for adults of legal cannabis age.

If you believe a minor has used Strain Diary in a way that involved their personal information, please visit the Contact page on the Strain Diary website so the issue can be reviewed.

Your control over your data

You can edit or delete your personal diary entries directly within Strain Diary, and applicable changes can sync across your devices through iCloud.

Because your personal diary is stored through your iCloud account, you can also manage applicable Strain Diary data through your Apple and iCloud settings.

Content you choose to share through Community or Chat may be handled differently from your private diary because those features require information to be shared with other users or the service.

If you need help managing or removing your information, please visit the Contactpage.

Changes to this policy

If Strain Diary's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated and the Last updated date at the top of this page will be changed.

Meaningful changes will be reflected here so you can understand what information Strain Diary handles and how it is used.
