Privacy policy

Your logs are yours.

Last updated August 31, 2026

Strain Diary is independently built and maintained by one person. Private diary entries are stored through Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit services. Your information is not sold or rented, and the app does not use advertising networks or third-party tracking SDKs.

Private diary data

Your private logs may include strain and product names, ratings, reviews, notes, effects, photos, session information, cannabinoid information, terpene data and other details you choose to save. Private entries are stored in your personal iCloud account through CloudKit and are not visible to other users.

There is no separate Strain Diary account required for your private diary. CloudKit associates this information with the Apple Account already signed in on your device and syncs it across your supported Apple devices. Apple handles the underlying storage and transfer under its applicable privacy practices and terms.

Community and shared strain data

If you contribute information to the community database, details such as a strain name, brand, crosses, terpenes and other product information may be stored in a shared database so they can help with search and autofill. Your private review, rating and notes are not added to the community strain database unless you deliberately share them.

Social features and chat

When you use community or chat features, the app may process usernames, profile information, profile photos, posts, comments, replies, message content, reactions, shared strain cards and photos you choose to send. This information is used to display posts, deliver messages, support conversations and operate the social features you requested.

Content only becomes publicly visible when you choose to share it publicly. Community, chat and diary content may be accessible to Strain Diary’s owner or authorized moderators when necessary for moderation, support, safety, troubleshooting or operation of the service.

Photos and camera access

Camera and photo access are used only when you choose to scan a label, attach a product photo, set a profile image or send a photo through a supported feature. The app does not browse or upload unrelated photos.

Connected devices

If you pair Strain Diary with a compatible device, that connection happens locally between your phone and the hardware. It is used to provide the device feature you requested and is not used to build an advertising profile.

Analytics and diagnostics

Strain Diary does not include third-party analytics SDKs that track your activity across apps. If you enable Share iPhone Analytics in iOS, Apple may provide anonymous or aggregate crash, performance and diagnostic information. This does not include the contents of your private strain journal. You can manage this setting under Privacy & Security on your device.

When you contact me

If you contact Strain Diary or send TestFlight feedback, I receive whatever you choose to include. Apple may also attach technical details such as your device model, operating system version or crash information. This is used to respond, troubleshoot and improve the app—not to add you to a marketing list.

Data sharing

Strain Diary does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, such as Apple’s CloudKit infrastructure. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or the public.

Your control over your data

You can edit or delete personal diary entries inside Strain Diary, and those changes can sync through iCloud. You can also manage applicable app data through your Apple and iCloud settings. Community and chat content works differently because those features require information to be shared with other users or the service. Contact support if you need help managing or removing shared information.

Adults only and children’s privacy

Strain Diary is intended only for adults of legal cannabis age. It is not directed to children, does not knowingly allow minors to use adult features and does not provide medical advice. If you believe a minor used Strain Diary in a way that involved personal information, please contact support.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as features are added or updated. The date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@straindiary.com or through the contact page.
