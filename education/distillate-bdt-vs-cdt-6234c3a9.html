Distillate: BDT vs. CDT 🌿

Garrett Goehring · August 21, 2026

Distillate is a highly refined cannabis extract that contains mostly THC. The refining process removes many of the plant’s natural terpenes, so terpenes are often added back for flavor and aroma.

🍓 BDT — Botanically Derived Terpenes
BDTs come from non-cannabis plants and are commonly used to create stronger, fruitier, or candy-like flavors. They’re generally cheaper and are common in flavored carts and disposables.

🌿 CDT — Cannabis-Derived Terpenes
CDTs are extracted directly from cannabis. They tend to provide a more natural, strain-like flavor and aroma that resembles actual flower.

The Bottom Line:
🍬 BDT = Terpenes from other plants, often sweeter/flavored
🌱 CDT = Terpenes from cannabis, more flower-like

Neither automatically means a product is better or safer—quality, formulation, and proper testing matter too.
