Live Rosin vs. Live Resin 🍯

Garrett Goehring · August 21, 2026

Both live rosin and live resin are cannabis concentrates made using fresh-frozen flower to help preserve the plant’s cannabinoids and terpenes. The biggest difference is how they’re extracted.

🧊 Live Rosin
Live rosin is solventless. Fresh-frozen cannabis is typically made into hash, then pressed using heat and pressure to extract the concentrate. Because no chemical solvents are used, live rosin is often considered a more premium concentrate and usually costs more.

🧪 Live Resin
Live resin uses solvents, commonly hydrocarbons such as butane or propane, to extract cannabinoids and terpenes from fresh-frozen cannabis. The solvents are then removed during processing. Properly produced and tested live resin should meet limits for residual solvents.

The Bottom Line:
🌿 Live Rosin = Solventless, heat + pressure
🧪 Live Resin = Solvent-based extraction

Both can offer strong effects and flavorful, terpene-rich experiences the better choice mostly comes down to preference and product quality.
